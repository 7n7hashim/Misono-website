Questions

Before you visit

If something here is not answered, the kitchen would rather you called than guessed.

Where is Li’s?

There are two, and both are in Petrocity buildings. Mombasa is on the first floor of Petrocity Plaza, Links Road, Nyali — that is the original. Nairobi is at Petrocity on Limuru Road in Gigiri.

Do I need a reservation?

Both branches take them, and they are worth making at the end of a week. If you want the Peking duck, say so when you book: it is not a dish that can be started when you sit down.

What kind of Chinese food do you serve?

Chinese cooking with a strong Indo-Chinese streak — dim sum and spring rolls to start, then the wok: chilli garlic, black bean, Schezwan, Kung Pao, and the sizzling platters. Seafood is the part the kitchen is proudest of, which is the coast showing.

Do you do Peking duck?

Yes, half or whole, carved and served with thin pancakes, cucumber and spring onion. It is the dish most people come for. Order it when you book rather than when you arrive.

Do you have vegetarian options?

A great many — a whole vegetable section, plus tofu and paneer done several ways, vegetable dim sum, spring rolls and soups. Halal, vegan and gluten-free are not something we can confirm here for either branch. For an allergy or anything more specific, please call the branch: the kitchen would rather tell you plainly what it can and cannot do than have you find out at the table.

Can you host a private dinner?

Mombasa has two private rooms, each seating around twelve to fifteen. Nairobi takes private-event enquiries too. Either way it is a conversation rather than a booking form — call the branch, or use the form above.

Do you deliver, and can I take away?

Yes to both. Takeaway from either branch, and delivery in Nairobi through Uber Eats.

What are your opening hours?

Both branches are closed on Mondays and open Tuesday to Sunday from 11:00. They close at different times: Nairobi at 22:30, Mombasa at 21:00. We serve straight through the afternoon, so a late lunch is as welcome as dinner.
